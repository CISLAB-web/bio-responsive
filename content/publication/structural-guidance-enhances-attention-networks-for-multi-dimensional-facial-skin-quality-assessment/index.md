abstract: >
  Accurate assessment of facial skin condition is essential for both
  dermatological diagnostics and the development of personalized skincare
  solutions. However, existing image-based methods are insufficiently sensitive
  to fine-scale variations and are

  easily affected by illumination. We propose SGA-U-Net, which couples structural guidance (SG) with structure-aware attention

  (SGA). Rule-based pseudo-labels of pores and wrinkles derived via classical image processing provide auxiliary supervision,

  and attention-augmented skip connections jointly predict four skin quality indicators, moisture, glossiness, sebum, and elasticity

  within a multi-task framework. Validated on our high-resolution, dual-illumination FaceSkin-Q dataset and the public Korean

  Skin Condition Measurement Dataset (NIA), the method consistently surpasses convolutional neural network (CNN) and

  Transformer baselines in both accuracy and illumination robustness. These results indicate that combining structural priors

  with attention enhances sensitivity to skin microstructures and improves cross-scene stability, suggesting potential utility for

  supporting future dermatological assessment and personalized skincare applications.
